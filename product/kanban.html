Fractional LexoRank Ordering

Drag-and-drop task reordering uses floating fractional position indices. Moving a task between position 1000 and 2000 assigns 1500, eliminating massive database write locks and table re-indexing delays.

Custom WIP Limits

Prevent sprint bottlenecks. Columns like "In Progress" or "Code Review" feature customizable WIP caps. Visual badges flash when team limits are exceeded.
